# Share an agent

A shared agent spreads expertise. Share yours once it works well on real tasks.

1. Open the agent in the **Agent Builder**.
2. Click **Share**.
3. Search for people or groups by name or email, and choose each one's **role**:

   | Role | Can |
   |---|---|
   | **Viewer** | Use the agent. Can't change it. |
   | **Editor** | Use and change the agent. |
   | **Manager** | Use, change and delete the agent. |
   | **Owner** | Everything, including sharing it with others. |

4. To publish it to the whole company, turn on **Share with everyone** and choose everyone's
   permission level, usually **Viewer**. Agents shared with everyone appear in the
   [Agent Marketplace](marketplace.md).

You can also **Copy Link** to send someone straight to the agent.

---

## Good sharing habits

- **Share with a group, not a list of people.** New team members then get access automatically.
- **Give Editor to one or two co-maintainers** so the agent doesn't depend on one person.
- **Fill in Support Contact** so users know who to ask.
- **Pick a category and write a clear description** before sharing with everyone.
- **Check what the agent can reach.** Anyone who uses a shared agent can get answers drawn from
  its knowledge files, so don't attach anything the audience shouldn't see.
- **Tell people about it.** Post the link in your team's channel with one example of what it
  does.
